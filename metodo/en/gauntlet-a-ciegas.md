# Blind gauntlet

When the question is "does this look or feel better?", we decide like this:

1. We put the new thing next to the old one, or next to a real yardstick (a published game or the studio's north video).
2. Three fresh critics, who do not know which is which, choose.
3. It goes in if it wins 2 of 3. If not, it does not go in, even if the machines say everything passes.

Example: the baked light in the boxes lost three attempts in a row and was stopped. The paper night star lost on Nov 6 and won 3 of 3 on Nov 8 with a different design.

The critic is never the one who built the piece.
