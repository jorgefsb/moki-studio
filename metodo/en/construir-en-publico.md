# Building in public

- We publish every day, on every network, with something you can see.
- Every post invites people to play at moki.jorgesuarez.com.mx and explains that we measure without anyone's data.
- One series a day at most.
- We tell what worked and what did not make it.
- Everything goes through the reviewer (fixed rules, a read of the video and a cheap critic), and it goes out no matter what: if it finds something, we fix it until it goes out (Jorge, Nov 9). See [[bitacora-viva]].
- Old versions are kept forever and can be played.
- What is already published is never deleted; we correct going forward.

## Documenting is creating (Jorge, Nov 9)

- Every entry tells a story, not a changelog: who wanted what, what went wrong, what we decided and why. With papacitoos-storytelling.
- It should sound like a person: escritura-humana, short sentences, no AI words.
- We measure what we publish: every link carries which network it came from and we count how many plays each post brought. Every Saturday we see what people liked (plays and retention, not just likes) and change one rule of each skill. If it does not improve, we roll it back.
