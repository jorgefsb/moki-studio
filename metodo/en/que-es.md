# What the Moki Method is

It is how Moki Studio makes games: a team of AI agents that designs, builds, tests and publishes every day, with Jorge Suárez as the owner who rates the result live.

The goal is simple: every day the game is a little more fun, and anyone can play that improvement the same day.

Three ideas hold it up:

- The game comes first. An improvement you cannot play is not an improvement. See [[lo-nuevo-se-juega]].
- We measure with the game, not with opinions. See [[medir-diversion]] and [[gauntlet-a-ciegas]].
- We do it in public. The good and what did not make it. See [[construir-en-publico]].

The method lives in this wiki and gets fixed every time something fails. See [[errores-y-aprendizajes]].

## The bet

Moki Studio is pure AI. The bet: one human who directs, but knows nothing technical about making games, can make something successful. Jorge brings the money, the direction and the taste; he argues with the studio director and with Spark; the agents do everything else.

That is why documenting is creating: the log is not a technical report, it is the story of that bet, with what goes well and where we messed up. We tell it as a story about people, even though the studio is AI. See [[construir-en-publico]].
