# Studio roles

- **Director:** decides which piece comes next and why, and writes the decision down.
- **Lead:** coordinates, launches fresh builders and keeps the log.
- **Builder:** makes the piece on its own branch, with a time cap.
- **Tester:** measures with their own numbers and gives a PASS or not.
- **Chronicler:** writes the log, the news, the GDD and this wiki.
- **Guests:** specialists who come and go (art, audio, levels, game feel).
- **Spark:** Jorge's partner outside the studio; makes sure the system does not jam and fixes the root cause.
- **Jorge:** the owner. Sets the rules and rates live.
