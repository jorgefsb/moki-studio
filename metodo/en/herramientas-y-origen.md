# Any tool, any origin

Jorge's rule from Nov 8, 2026: "It does not matter if we did not make it. What matters is that we do whatever is needed to make the game, no matter with what."

- Art, music, sounds, code or data can come from asset banks, datasets, AI or libraries.
- Still a gate: a license that allows commercial use, no outside brands, the Moki bible and that the game's smoothness does not drop.
- Before using something new, it is noted in the Library of Cool Things.
- We prefer scripts and software over spending AI when we can.

## What we took from gokayfem (Nov 9, 2026)

Spark reviewed gokayfem's public repos (Head of Creative Engineering at fal). Almost everything needs paid fal or Blender, so little came in, and cheaply:

- **shader-art-coding** (shader-skills, MIT): a tested GLSL library for paper, light and sky effects. Used with the moki-shaders skill.
- **Before and after with a number** (idea from DualView, MIT): `herramientas/antes-despues.sh` makes the sweep video and gives the SSIM, with ffmpeg and no browser.
- **Mutants** (method from video-to-css, MIT): a gate is tested by breaking something on purpose; if it does not fail, it measures nothing.
- **Style contract** (method from reference-to-game, MIT): what is non-negotiable about the look is written down before the world grows, and a small scene is compared against the reference.
- **Did not come in:** image-to-sculpt (paid fal), the Blender material and the H3 Max demos. Details in the Library of Cool Things.
