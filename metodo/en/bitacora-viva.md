# Bitácora Viva

Bitácora Viva is the system we use to tell, in public, how Moki is made day by day, and to measure which story reached people. It was born here, at Moki Studio, in November 2026. It is built to work just as well with another game, other software or any project built in plain sight.

## The three pieces

- **Lane.** Each project has one: what it is, where it is going, which site it lives on, which networks it goes out to and what material it can talk about. A lane never sees another lane's data.
- **Border.** A reviewer without AI reads every text before it goes out: it must carry no data that is not ours, no secrets, no links to another project, and every link must say which network it comes from.
- **Cycle.** Publish, measure, learn and change one rule a week. Every Saturday we see which post brought people who stayed to play, and we adjust how we tell the story.

## What you see on this site thanks to it

- The thumbnail gallery on the front page: every improvement leaves its image.
- The log with a story, charts, what went wrong and a question for you.
- The live [[medir-diversion]] and the ideas board where you vote on what comes next.
- Every link on social networks carries its origin tag. That is how we know which network brings people who really play, without storing anything about you.

## Its rules

- Documenting is creating: we tell stories about people and decisions, not lists of changes. See [[construir-en-publico]].
- It goes out no matter what: if the reviewer finds something, we fix it until it goes out.
- What is new can be played: if we announce it, it has to be playable that same day. See [[lo-nuevo-se-juega]].
