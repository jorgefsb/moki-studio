# Measuring fun

The game counts plays anonymously: no accounts, no tracking cookies, no personal data.

- Public, team and bots are kept apart.
- We count where each play ends, how many leave before 10 seconds and how many ask for another.
- When few people play, we measure with bots and with a simulated first-time player. We do not wait days for the sample to arrive.

Every publication says it: "We count plays without your data. The more you play, the better we know what to fix."

All of this is live at https://moki.jorgesuarez.com.mx/termometro/: the big thermometer is the percentage of plays that ask for another (goal 50), with cards for the first 10 seconds and the curve, a chart per day with the versions and the days we messed up, and the decisions we made with those numbers.
