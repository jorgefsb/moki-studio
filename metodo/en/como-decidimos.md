# How we decide

- **Done beats perfect.** The team decides, implements, publishes and writes its postmortem. Jorge rates afterwards.
- **The 30-minute rule.** Every question to Jorge comes with a recommendation and a deadline. If he does not answer in 30 minutes, we follow the recommendation.
- **Exceptions:** money, contracts, deleting without a backup, secrets, real people without permission and anything that crosses companies wait for Jorge.
- **Every change has a way back:** backup, how to revert, live verification and a postmortem.
